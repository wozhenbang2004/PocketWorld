Windows native pairing QR display
=================================

python-qrcode 8.2 (Lincoln Loop), BSD-3-Clause with original pyqrnative/MIT attribution.
https://github.com/lincolnloop/python-qrcode/tree/v8.2
See python-qrcode-LICENSE.txt for the exact distribution license and attribution.

Colorama 0.4.6 (Jonathan Hartley), BSD-3-Clause.
https://github.com/tartley/colorama/tree/0.4.6
See colorama-LICENSE.txt. It is a Windows dependency of python-qrcode; this
application does not initialize its console wrapping or add an image-factory extra.

No Pillow or pypng QR dependency, telemetry, network encoder or QR URL carrier is used.
The complete shared PairingInvitation is encoded without modification.
